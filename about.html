About VAWnet

A free library, built for the people doing the work.

VAWnet is a project of the National Resource Center on Domestic Violence — an online library of thousands of materials on domestic violence, sexual violence and related issues.

Our story

Reviewed, tagged and kept current.

The library has been maintained since 1998 by librarians and technical assistance specialists at the NRCDV. Every item is reviewed before it is added, tagged against the same topic framework, and dated so you can see how current it is.

Special Collections go a step further: they gather the most useful materials on a single subject, written up by a subject specialist and refreshed as the field changes.

  • Nothing behind a paywall
  • No login needed to read
  • Every item dated and credited
  • Spanish and plain-language versions linked
