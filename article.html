TA Question of the MonthSeptember 16, 2026 · 4 min read

What are some ways that I can take action this Domestic Violence Awareness Month?

Survivors deserve action. This TA Question of the Month offers opportunities for taking action around this year's theme for Domestic Violence Awareness Month: “With Survivors, Always.”

This year's Domestic Violence Awareness Month theme — With Survivors, Always — asks us to move past awareness alone. Awareness without action leaves survivors carrying the same barriers in November that they carried in October.

Start close to home

Audit one policy at your own organization that makes help harder to reach: intake hours that assume a nine-to-five job, documentation requirements that assume stable housing, or a waiting list with no way to check your place in it. Changing one of those is worth more than a month of posts.

Then look outward

Ask local employers about paid safe leave, ask your city about funding for survivor-centred housing, and ask your coalition where advocacy capacity is thinnest this year. Bring survivors into those conversations as advisors with their expertise compensated, not as case studies.

Awareness without action leaves survivors carrying the same barriers in November that they carried in October.

Sustain the people doing the work

Vicarious trauma is an occupational reality, not a personal failing — build reflective supervision and real time off into the plan before the month begins.
