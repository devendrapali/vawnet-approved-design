Disaster and Emergency Preparedness and Response

Selected materials and resources — many gender-informed — that domestic and sexual violence organizations can use to increase preparedness for and response to major disasters and emergencies.

Read the material Download PDF · 1.4 MB

In this collection

Disasters and emergencies disrupt the systems survivors rely on — housing, income, childcare, protective orders and the advocacy programs themselves. This collection gathers materials that help organizations prepare before an event, respond during one, and support survivors through the long recovery that follows.

Resources are grouped into four parts:

  1. Continuity of operations for advocacy programs
  2. Safety planning during evacuation and displacement
  3. Coordinating with emergency management and mass shelters
  4. Research on gender-based violence after disaster

How to use it

Each item is summarised, dated and credited to its publisher so you can judge its currency before you use it. Where a resource exists in Spanish or in plain language, that version is linked alongside the original.

Preparedness planning works best when survivors and advocates are at the table before the emergency, not after it.
