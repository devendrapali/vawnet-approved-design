Training InstituteThursday, October 22, 2026 · 1:00 – 2:30 PM ET

Back to Basics: Economic Justice for Survivors

Practical guidance on tax credits, public benefits navigation and asset building — and how advocates can fold economic safety into everyday safety planning.

Register free Add to calendar

About this session

Economic abuse occurs in the vast majority of abusive relationships, and financial insecurity is one of the most common reasons survivors stay or return. This Training Institute session walks through the economic supports advocates are most often asked about, and how to raise them early.

What you'll learn

  1. How the EITC and Child Tax Credit reach survivors — and where they don't
  2. Navigating public benefits without compromising safety
  3. Talking about credit, debt and coerced debt with survivors
  4. Building economic safety into your safety-planning practice

Who should attend

Advocates and direct service staff, economic justice program leads, and anyone at a coalition or program who supports survivors with housing, income or benefits. No prior knowledge is assumed.
